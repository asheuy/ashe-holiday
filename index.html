Field notes

Wisdom for the road

01

Choose your season

November to April offers warmer surf and easier alpine roads. Winter rewards flexible travelers with snow and quieter towns.

02

Respect the distances

New Zealand roads are scenic and winding. Add time for viewpoints, weather and the sheep that occasionally command the road.

03

Book the icons early

Reserve Hobbiton, Maori cultural evenings, Milford cruises and Tekapo stargazing before premium time slots disappear.

04

Carry four seasons

Pack layers, waterproof shells and sun protection. Alpine conditions can change before the second breakfast arrives.

05

Travel with care

Follow Tiaki principles: protect nature, drive thoughtfully and approach places of Maori significance with respect.

06

Keep the reserve

The $1,170 buffer covers one-way car fees, fuel changes, weather pivots and a few unexpectedly excellent meals.
